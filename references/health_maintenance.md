# Health Maintenance / Preventive Care Checklist

Use this for cases explicitly framed as a periodic/annual/health-maintenance visit, or when the user brings up preventive care during another case and it's fair to grade. **Use judgment**: for a station built around an acute or red-flag-heavy complaint, real OSCEs generally don't expect a candidate to also run the full preventive-care checklist -- don't penalize a candidate for staying focused on an acute complaint. If you deliberately leave health maintenance out of a case's rubric for this reason, say so briefly in the debrief.

Always weigh age, sex, and comorbidities when deciding which items actually apply to a given patient -- an item that doesn't apply to the patient's age/sex/risk profile shouldn't appear on that case's checklist at all.

These summarize general US preventive-care recommendations (USPSTF for screening, ACIP for vaccines) as of 2026. Thresholds change; when in doubt, credit a candidate who raises the right item at roughly the right age or risk level rather than demanding an exact interval.

## Cardiovascular and metabolic
- Blood pressure screening for all adults
- Lipid screening, and a statin discussion for adults about 40-75 with cardiovascular risk factors and an elevated 10-year risk
- Diabetes/prediabetes screening for adults about 35-70 with overweight or obesity
- Aspirin for primary prevention only as an individualized decision in adults 40-59 with elevated cardiovascular risk; not started routinely at 60 or older

## Cancer screening
- **Colorectal:** ages 45-75; individualized at 76-85
- **Breast:** mammography every 2 years from 40 to 74
- **Cervical:** cytology every 3 years at 21-29; at 30-65, cytology every 3 years, HPV testing every 5 years, or co-testing every 5 years
- **Lung:** yearly low-dose CT at 50-80 with at least 20 pack-years who smoke now or quit within the past 15 years
- **Prostate:** PSA as a shared decision at 55-69, not a default order; not routinely after 70

## Other screening
- **Osteoporosis:** DEXA for women 65 and older, and younger postmenopausal women at increased risk
- **Abdominal aortic aneurysm:** one-time ultrasound for men 65-75 who have ever smoked
- **HIV:** at least once for adolescents and adults about 15-65, and more often if at higher risk
- **Hepatitis C:** at least once for adults 18-79
- **STIs:** chlamydia/gonorrhea, syphilis and hepatitis B based on age and risk
- **Behavioral:** tobacco use (with cessation help), unhealthy alcohol use, and depression

## Vaccines
- Influenza every year
- Td/Tdap booster every 10 years (Tdap once as an adult if never given)
- Recombinant zoster (Shingrix) at 50 and older
- Pneumococcal at 50 and older, or younger with high-risk conditions (product and timing rules change, so credit raising it at the right age or risk)
- HPV routinely through 26; shared decision at 27-45
- COVID-19 per current seasonal guidance

## Grading notes

- Give credit for raising the *right* items for this specific patient's age/sex/risk profile -- don't expect every item on this list for every patient.
- A candidate correctly declining to bring up an irrelevant item (e.g. mammography for a 30-year-old with no risk factors) is not a miss.
- If dates/results are already documented in the case as up to date, a good candidate should note that rather than re-ordering unnecessarily.
